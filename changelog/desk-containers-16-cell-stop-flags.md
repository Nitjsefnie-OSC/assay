### Added
- Cell-scoped stop flags: `STOP.cell.<cell>` stops every desk loop of one cell and `STOP.cell.<cell>.<loop>` one loop of it, where a session belongs to a cell only through the `ASSAY_CELL` marker `cellctl` sets. The Guard refuses every outward verb under either flag, and the `pr-review-desk`, `verify-desk` and `worker-desk` iteration-boundary checks read them too. Cell flags only add stops (precedence `DISABLED` > `STOP` > `STOP.cell.<cell>` > `STOP.<loop>` = `STOP.cell.<cell>.<loop>`); a marker outside `[a-z0-9-]` is refused exit 6 before any path is built; `deskboard` lists cell flags and tags one INERT when its cell has no registration under the cells root.
