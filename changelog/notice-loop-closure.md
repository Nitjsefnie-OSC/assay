### Changed
- `statusgen` board-drift notices now reach the transition that acts on them: a todo row delivered by a merged `Brief:`-trailer PR, or re-homed by a pointer row, is held out of the Next-up and dispatch queues instead of being re-dispatched. A two-parent merge reads its `Brief:` trailers from the branch commits whatever the branch is called (one batched `git log` for the window). An `Authors:` trailer never counts as delivery, a merge read that fails stays could-not-check, and text-heuristic classes (a brief that merely discusses de-housing) stay notice-only.
- `statusgen --register-links` repairs a typed `[F-NN]`/`[I-NN]` link to the generated view when exactly one register entry carries the id, and lists ambiguous or unmapped links as author-owed without changing them. Recorded Evidence (both the link repair and the bare-id linking skip it), Verify commands and fenced text (backtick or tilde fences) are never rewritten.
- The `verified-runner-attribution` notice names the cell the accepted run record derives, so a historical mismatch points at the record rather than the caller.
