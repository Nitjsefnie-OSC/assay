### Changed
- `deskevidence` now owns the on-behalf-of principal on the Runner cells of App-run Evidence rows a landing adds. It reads an existing annotation the way the attribution lint does. A cell carrying exactly the stamp the tool writes, naming the roster's principal, is left as it is. A planted or non-principal annotation, including a desk role, an unknown name, the same human in the other form, or the principal in another spelling, is stripped and restamped in place, keeping any source tag after it. A row naming a different roster human is refused, and nothing is written. So is a row whose Runner cell the lint would read differently from its text: an HTML comment in the cell or across the row's line break, invalid UTF-8, an invisible format character, or a character whose lowercase form changes byte length.
- `deskevidence --dry-run` prints each row the stamp wrote. The verify-desk skill now says to leave Runner cells unannotated and to pre-check with `--dry-run`.
