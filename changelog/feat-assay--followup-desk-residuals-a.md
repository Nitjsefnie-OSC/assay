### Fixed
- `deskevidence` now requires the Evidence section a landing adds to be one a Markdown renderer and the lint read the same way, before it checks or stamps any on-behalf-of principal. Added lines must be blank lines, `|`-led rows under a header with a delimiter row a renderer accepts, or note lines that show no App-run or on-behalf-of text. Only the cell the lint reads as Runner may show as an App-run Runner cell. A Runner cell that shows as App-run once its HTML, entity, emphasis, code-span or escape markup is removed must also read as App-run to the lint. Headings shown as Evidence outside the one section the lint reads (ATX, setext or HTML, with entities decoded) take no new rows. Rows outside that section may not show as App-run. The checks are rules held by the tool rather than a full Markdown parse, and the lint-way read of the landed section stays the last check. Nothing is written when a landing is refused.
- The `--brief-path` merge now puts a blank line before an appended block that opens with a table header, so a witness table appended after an attestation line starts its own table.
